About Me

I am an AI Engineer with a Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Bologna (110/110 cum laude). I currently work as an AI Engineer at LAIF and as a Consultant AI Engineer at CRIF, building production AI/backend systems and LLM-powered solutions (RAG, multi-agent systems) on the OpenAI and AWS Bedrock stack. I am passionate about turning cutting-edge AI into robust, practical software.

My work focuses on building scalable backend systems and integrating AI capabilities into production applications. I also build personal projects: LayerCost, a live 3D-printing quote calculator, and Moth, a small Italian/English speech-to-text model designed for the browser.
